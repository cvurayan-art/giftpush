Every Question, Answered.

Everything you need to know about crafting, sizing, and caring for your DIY Cat Bed.

Zero experience needed! This kit is designed specifically for complete beginners. You do not need needles or crochet hooks — you simply use your hands and fingers. The included step-by-step 4K video guide walks you through every single loop at a slow, comfortable pace.
Most makers finish their bed in approximately 35 to 50 minutes. It is a relaxing, therapeutic craft that can easily be completed in one sitting over a cup of tea.
Yes, 100%. Our chunky yarn is spun from high-grade hypoallergenic braided cotton without harmful chemical dyes, fire retardants, or scratchy fibers. It's gentle on sensitive paws and noses.
The finished bed is 100% machine washable! Simply pop it into the machine on a gentle cold or 30°C cycle with mild detergent, then reshape slightly and let it air dry flat. It retains its structure and plushness wash after wash.
The finished bed measures approximately 40cm x 40cm with a 30cm height (flexible depending on loop tension), comfortably accommodating kittens, adult cats, and small pets up to 8.5 kg (18.7 lbs).
