Heirloom Quality Made For Generations

Every Engraved Keepsake Box begins as select American walnut, harvested from sustainably managed forests in the Midwest. Each piece of wood features unique swirls, deep cocoa hues, and golden growth rings that make no two boxes completely identical.

Our master craftsmen sand each box through four grades of grit before applying a natural beeswax and organic mineral oil finish that protects the wood while highlighting its organic warmth. Antique brushed brass hinges provide an effortless 95-degree soft-stop opening.

Personalization & Engraving FAQ

How is it engraved? We use high-precision CO2 laser engraving that deeply burns the design into the hardwood fibers. It will never fade, peel, or rub off.

Can I add custom characters or symbols? Yes! We support ampersands (&), hearts (♥), Roman numerals, and international accents.

Proofing Guarantee: Our in-house designers inspect every order for optical centering and baseline balance prior to engraving. If we spot an obvious spelling typo, our concierge team reaches out immediately.
