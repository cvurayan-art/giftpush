HELP & INSPIRATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to common questions about choosing, personalizing, and delivering your holiday gifts.

What is a good Christmas gift for someone who has everything?

For someone who has everything, personalized gifts and shared experiences create lasting sentiment. An engraved family keepsake box, custom memory ornament, or a luxury gourmet gift hamper provides emotional value that off-the-shelf items cannot replicate.

What are good personalized Christmas gifts?

Popular personalized gifts include custom-engraved wooden keepsake boxes, personalized ceramic holiday mugs with custom names and dates, laser-etched family tree ornaments, and monogrammed holiday journals.

How early should I order Christmas gifts?

We recommend ordering by December 10th for standard delivery and December 16th for express shipping to ensure safe arrival before Christmas morning.

Does Never The Wrong Gift offer gift wrapping?

Yes, every gift can be wrapped in our signature forest green paper, tied with a burgundy silk ribbon and evergreen pine sprig, complete with a personalized wax-sealed handwritten message card.

Can I add a personalized message?

Yes. You can add a custom note, recipient name, or special date during checkout, and our artisans will hand-inscribe it onto our keepsake greeting card free of charge.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days within the continental United States. Express shipping delivers within 1–2 business days. Orders over $75 include free standard shipping.

Can Christmas gifts be returned?

Yes, non-personalized gifts can be returned or exchanged within 30 days of delivery. For holiday orders placed in November and December, our extended return window lasts until January 31st.
