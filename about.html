Our Story

Thoughtful Gifting, Made Simple

Present Panic was born from a simple belief: finding the right gift shouldn’t feel stressful. It should feel exciting, personal, and memorable.

Curated with Purpose

Every product in our catalog is hand-selected or custom crafted to bring genuine joy to both the giver and the recipient. From handcrafted solid wood keepsake boxes to artisan holiday candles and refined lifestyle essentials, we focus on heirloom quality rather than throwaway novelties.

Whether you need a personalized wedding gift, a holiday thank-you hamper, or a spontaneous gesture of appreciation, our collections are organized to help you choose with confidence.

Authentic Craftsmanship

Real materials, precise laser engravings, and rigorous quality inspection on every shipment.

Frictionless Commerce

100% guest checkout powered by Shopify. No forced account creation or password hurdles.

Tracked Delivery

Carefully packaged to arrive in gift-ready presentation, with automated tracking from order to doorstep.
