Order Processing Timelines

Standard Non-Customized Items

Dispatched within 1–2 business days from our fulfillment center. Orders placed before 1:00 PM EST typically ship same-day.

Personalized & Laser-Crafted Items

Require an additional 24–48 hours of workshop production time for laser engraving, hand buffing, and careful inspection before dispatch.

Order Tracking

You will receive an automated shipping confirmation email with your official carrier tracking number (USPS, FedEx, or UPS) the moment your shipping label is generated. Tracking updates typically begin reflecting scans within 12–24 hours.

Gift-Ready Packaging

All orders are packed securely in protective exterior shipping cartons with tissue wrap. Packing slips never include item prices, making it completely safe to ship gifts directly to the recipient’s address.
