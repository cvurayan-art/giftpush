Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy outlines how your information is handled during your visits and guest purchases at Present Panic.

1. Information We Collect

Present Panic is designed as a guest-first commerce experience. We do not require or operate user accounts or customer login portals.

When you place a guest order, we collect only the necessary information required to fulfill your purchase and provide delivery tracking:

  • Recipient Details: Name, delivery shipping address, and phone number for delivery couriers.
  • Contact Email: Used strictly to send automated order receipts, personalization confirmations, and dispatch tracking links.
  • Customization Text: Any personalization, name, or dedication message you type for custom-crafted items.

2. Payment Security & Shopify Infrastructure

All payment card transactions and checkout processes are executed directly by Shopify on level-1 PCI-DSS compliant secure infrastructure.

Present Panic never captures, receives, or stores credit card numbers, CVV codes, or full banking credentials on our web servers. Payments are encrypted end-to-end using industry-standard TLS encryption.

3. Local Storage & Essential Cookies

We use browser local storage solely to preserve your active shopping bag identifier (present_panic_shopify_cart_id). This ensures your selected items and custom engraving details remain saved as you navigate between product pages.

We do not sell customer personal data to third-party data brokers or marketing aggregators.

4. Your Rights & Inquiries

You may request confirmation of any order data associated with your email address or request deletion of order communications by contacting our privacy coordinator at privacy@presentpanic.com.
